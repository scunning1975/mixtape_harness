# Miller, Johnson & Wherry (2021) — Summary pp. 21–24

## What these pages argue
These pages bound the compliers' counterfactual mortality rate, translate reduced-form effects into implied treatment effects on the treated, compare those to prior experimental (OHIE, Goldin et al.) and quasi-experimental literature, and conclude with the lives-saved / cost-of-non-expansion calculations.

## Key concepts / equations
- Complier counterfactual bounds: lower = all-sample 1.63%; upper = enrollee-based 2.95% (= 2.0 + 2.7 × 0.35).
- Implied treatment effects = reduced-form ÷ coverage change, expressed as % of counterfactual.
- Lives-saved calculation from population × per-year mortality reduction.

## Main numbers / results
- Enrollee post-period mortality = 2.0% (upper-bound input); compliers stay enrolled ~2.7 years.
- One year of Medicaid coverage reduces mortality **11.9–21.5%** (using 0.35pp effect over 1.63–2.95% counterfactual).
- Contemporaneous Medicaid enrollment (1.03pp) → **34.9–63.2%** reduction.
- Scaling by net insurance (+4.4pp) → 102–184% (implausibly large → private/Medicaid differences matter).
- OHIE (ages 55–64): ever-Medicaid cut mortality 1.65pp = 71.7% vs. control (p=0.128, ns); annualized 1.24pp. Insurance-based: 326% (p=0.071) / 335% (p=0.153).
- Goldin et al. (2021): each month coverage -0.178pp (10.1%); 12 months → 106%.
- Quasi-experimental: Sommers (2017) 64.5%; Sommers et al. (2014) 27.3%; Borgschulte-Vogler (2020) 47.3%; this paper ages 19–64 = 50% (ns); Chen (2019) ~9% (ns). Range 9–65%.
- **Lives saved: ~4,800 fewer deaths/year in expansion states; ~19,200 over 4 years** (3.7M population).
- **Non-expansion cost: ~15,600 avoidable deaths over 4 years** (3M population).

### DiD ELEMENTS
1. **BITE (first stage):** Referenced for scaling — accumulated 0.375 Medicaid years; contemporaneous Medicaid +12.8pp; net insurance +4.4pp. Compliers gain ~2.7 years of coverage.
2. **FALSIFICATIONS:** External-cause exclusion reiterated; adverse-selection caveat (enrollees sicker, Garthwaite et al. 2019 higher pre-ACA ED/hospital use) frames why experimental effects exceed control mean.
3. **EVENT STUDIES:** None new on these pages.
4. **MAIN RESULTS:** Reduced-form -0.132pp → **4,800 lives/year, 19,200 over 4 years**; non-expansion **15,600 deaths**. Implied per-life mortality effects 11.9–21.5% (accumulated) / 34.9–63.2% (contemporaneous Medicaid).
5. **MECHANISM:** Health channel via effective medical care; Medicaid covers long-term/home-community services (Rudowitz 2019); $3,100/yr out-of-pocket savings (McInerney 2020) = financial channel. Estimates smaller than experimental, larger than quasi-experimental literature.

### DATA DETAILS
- CMS enrollment data (no eligibility pathway → upper-bound caveat; excludes SSI recipients per sample criteria). OHIE public-use replication kit (Finkelstein et al. 2012 surveys ~1 and ~13 months post-approval). ACS used to count ~3.7M (expansion) and ~3M (non-expansion) eligible populations. Lives-saved assumes parallel trends counterfactual.
