# Cengiz, Dube, Lindner & Zipperer (2019) — Summary pp. 5–8 (QJE pp. 1409–1412)

**What these pages argue.** Completes the Introduction (heterogeneity, sectors, contributions, roadmap) and begins Section II (Methodology/Conceptual Framework). Two threads: (1) the null overall effect does not mask offsetting heterogeneity or labor-labor substitution; (2) the bin-by-bin approach exposes why classic two-way fixed effects (TWFE) produces spurious disemployment.

**Key concepts/results.**
- **Kaitz index** (min-to-median wage): event-by-event, missing jobs rise with the Kaitz index but excess jobs rise nearly identically → **no relationship between net employment and the Kaitz index up to ~59%**; US minimum wages haven't reached a disemployment threshold.
- **No labor-labor substitution**: education × age partitioning shows no swap of low- for high-skill. Card-Krueger (1995) predicted-exposure groups (high/med/low) all near zero, insignificant — benefits shared broadly.
- **Sectoral**: tradeable sector, esp. **manufacturing**, own-wage elasticity ≈ **−1.4** (imprecise); nontradeable (restaurants, retail) ≈ **0**. Industry composition matters.
- **TWFE critique**: Meer & West (2016)'s large negative effect, decomposed by bins, is driven by an implausibly large **upper-tail** drop; driven **entirely by the 1980s/early-1990s** inclusion (few MW changes; correlated with 2000s changes). Post-1992, little teen disemployment across specs.
- **Inequality lit**: uses frequency distribution (not density as in DiNardo-Fortin-Lemieux 1996; Lee 1999; Autor-Manning-Smith 2016) to assess inequality + employment jointly; spillovers not a disemployment artifact. Spillovers similar to Autor-Manning-Smith (2016), Brochu et al. (2017); smaller than Lee (1999).
- **Bunching lit**: uses DiD (not cross-sectional as Saez 2010; Chetty et al. 2013) for the counterfactual.

**Figures/tables.**
- **Figure I** (p. 1412): "The Impact of Minimum Wages on the Frequency Distribution of Wages." Red dashed = pre-MW; blue solid = post-MW. Missing jobs (Δb, red striped below MW); excess jobs above (Δa, blue between MW and W̄); Δe = Δa + Δb.
