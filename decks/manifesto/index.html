Design Trumps Analysis

and a harness to make me live by it
Scott Cunningham · a manifesto
The claim

Objective causal inference is built at the design stage — before you ever see the outcome.

Not recovered later by a clever regression. Manufactured up front, by refusing to look.
Rubin · 2008
“Final outcome data cannot be used in design without compromising the objectivity of the study design.”
Donald B. Rubin — “For Objective Causal Inference, Design Trumps Analysis”
Why blindness is objectivity

A randomized experiment can't be tuned. An observational study inherits that trust only by hiding the outcome.

Design — outcomes hidden
name the experiment · assignment · balance · the bite
Analysis — outcomes revealed
estimate the effect · last, and least
Rubin's design, in one line

Design isn't a step. It's six — and you take every one with the outcome hidden.

Miss any of the six, or peek at the outcome, and the objectivity is gone.
The six design steps

Everything you do before you look

1Name the experimentthe treatment, the outcome, the lost randomization you're rebuilding
2Sample sizes — firstpower & the ratios for well-matched samples, before you plunge in
3Strip the outcomeshide every Y until the design is done
4Find the decision-makerswho assigned treatment, and why — those drivers are the key covariates
5Measure them wellno amount of fancy analysis salvages a bad database
6Achieve balance — or refuserestrict to a subpopulation, or walk away from the data

The row that matters

Bite — the first stage. Proof the treatment moved something real, shown before the outcome.

Same object in every design. Turn the bite off, the effect vanishes. No bite, no interpretable study.

Then something changed

My new projects were where it broke.

Old projects I picked up — I knew those cold. But a new project is built by the checklist, step by step. That's the discipline agents quietly eroded.
The psychology of it

The agent severed production from verification — and from understanding.

200 lines in one shot. I approved work I hadn't produced and didn't fully hold. Little things kept breaking down.

Two quieter losses

My old workflow built a felt sense of the work — where every number came from. This one didn't stick.
